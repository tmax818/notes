* appendix: glossary of GTD terms

** actionable
something on which one intends to take action


** action support


** appropriate engagement

the statea of being sufficiently OK with something's status to eliminate its pull on one's attention.

** backlog

the inventory of still unprocessed stuff that has accumulated in mind and physical environment.

** capture
to gather/generate items/ideas that are potentially meaningful and actionable. see [[collect]]

** categories

** clarify

to determine exactly the meaning of something that has emerged in the environment from the [[capture]] phase.

** collect
to group items/ideas which require some assessment, decision or action. see [[capture]]

** context
phyiscal or psychological environment where reminders and informationn can be effectively sorted and accessed.

** horizontal thinking
assessing and managing content across a particular equal level(e.g. overviewing all the projects on has)

** map
any tool for orienting appropriate focus and direction
+ calendar
+ list of actions
+ list of projects
+ agendas
+ strategic plans
+ checklists
+ weekly review chedklist
  

** organize

to physically, visually, or digitally sort items of similar meanning into discrete categories and locations
+ lists

** perspective

** predefined work


** process

to decide what a captured or collected item means, the nature of its possible derivatives, and actions to take on results. see [[clarify]]
** project
any muiltstep outcome that can be completed within one year.
any commitment within that time frame needs to be reviewed at least weekly

** reflect
to assess contents of any horizon or category of items from a broader prespective

** review
to analyze appropriate maps on a consistent bases or as needed for claritgy and focus

** stuff

anything in physical or psychological environment which requires a decision or action that is yet undetermined or unorganized.
